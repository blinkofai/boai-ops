How a brief becomes a delivered, paid asset

The process map and system design for the account managers, editors and founders. The unit of work is the asset: every video, static and carousel, including each adapt and translation, gets its own ID, status, version history, delivery record and billing flag.

2 founders2 account managers: Padma, Tejita4 editors≈ ₹12 L / monthMany clients, AMCs and others
Where work comes from

Three intake streams, one pipeline

Retainers

HDFC Tuesday Talking Point (TTP) and Weekend Bytes. Billed per week. The system creates each week's slot ahead of time, so a brief that hasn't arrived is visible instead of being forgotten.

Series / projects

A batch of assets sold as one chunk. Tracked as an engagement with a total count, for example "14 of 20 delivered".

Ad hoc

One-off videos, statics or carousels from AMCs and other clients. Each request becomes a brief with one or more assets.

Per asset: video ₹20,000 · carousel ₹8,000 · static ₹3,000  |  Per week: TTP ₹45,000 · Weekend Bytes ₹64,000

Status values

Every asset sits in exactly one stage

These become the columns on the account managers' board. Amber stages are waiting on the client, and the system times them.

Brief logged→Info missing→Scripting→Script with client→Ready to assign→In production→Quality check→With client reviewer (vN)→Revising→Approved→Delivered→Invoiced→Paid

WhatsApp

Capturing the chats

  • A dedicated Blink of AI Ops number joins every client group. You map each group to a client once in the app, and from then on every message and file in that group is logged against that client.
  • Claude reads new messages and tags each one as a new brief, feedback on an asset, an approval, or general chat. New briefs become draft assets for an AM to confirm.

Limit: the bridge can only see chats the ops number is part of. Briefs sent to someone's personal WhatsApp stay invisible unless they're forwarded. Ask clients to use the group, and make "forward it to Ops" a team habit. One forward is enough for Claude to log the brief.

  4. Weeks 7–8: Zoho and Outlook sync, the 8 am brief, and the Claude connector.
Files

Getting out of the Drive / OneDrive mess

  • From go-live: every new version is uploaded once, to the asset in the app. The app stores and names it (HDFC-TTP-26W42-01_v2.mp4), and the delivery step pushes finals to the client's OneDrive or Drive when they need it there.
  • Old files: don't migrate everything. Import only active engagements and the last 3 months of finals. Leave the archive where it is and link to it from the client record.
  • Working files (project files, raw footage) can stay on whichever drive the editors use. The app only needs the exported versions.

Decisions

Settled so far

  • The account managers proofread and check every version before the client sees it.
  • Retainers are billed per week delivered: 5 deliveries in a month means 5 weeks billed.
  • The apps run on subdomains of blinkofai.io: ops. for the team and deliver. for clients.
  • Built in-house with Claude Code. The spec and database schema are in the project repo.
