Needs a person to check

  • India map (33–46 s): check that the northern border matches the official map of India.
  • Script: we didn't have the approved script, so nobody has compared the words on screen with it yet.
  • Voice-over: we couldn't tell if there is spoken audio. If there is, the risk warning may also need to be read out.
  • Length: we don't know which project this is for (TTP or a one-off), so we didn't check the length.

What's fine

  • Picture size, frame rate and file type are correct.
  • No black frames, glitches or silent gaps. The 7-second still at the end is intended.
  • The fund name "HDFC Innovation Fund" is spelled correctly.
  • The fund's own disclaimer is there and the wording is correct.
  • The innovation ranking at the start has its source.
  • No banned words like "guaranteed returns".
  • Text stays clear of the areas Instagram covers with buttons.
  • Hindi text displays correctly.
  • Car number plates are blank, and the glass car has no real brand logo.
Some rules are still missing from our HDFC rulebook: the exact Hindi wording of the risk warning, how long it must stay on screen, the logo files and where the riskometer comes from. Once those are filled in, we can check these items in more detail.
